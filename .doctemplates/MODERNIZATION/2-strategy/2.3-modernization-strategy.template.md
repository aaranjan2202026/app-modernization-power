**Tips for effective modernization planning:**

- Start with comprehensive current state assessment - don't skip this step
- Involve business stakeholders early and often
- Base recommendations on data and analysis, not assumptions
- Consider incremental approaches over big-bang migrations
- Plan for business continuity throughout the modernization
- Budget adequate time for testing and validation
- Include training and change management in your planning
- Document decision rationale for future reference
- Build in contingency for unknowns (typically 20-30%)
- Establish clear success criteria and measure continuously

**Common pitfalls to avoid:**

- ❌ Underestimating complexity of legacy code dependencies
- ❌ Insufficient testing and validation periods
- ❌ Neglecting data migration complexity
- ❌ Inadequate training and change management
- ❌ Lack of clear rollback procedures
- ❌ Overlooking integration impacts
- ❌ Unrealistic timelines without buffer
- ❌ Insufficient stakeholder engagement
