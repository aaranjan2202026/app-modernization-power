## Template Usage Notes

**For Documentation Compiler Agent**:

1. **Disclaimer Enforcement**: EVERY estimate section MUST have disclaimer explaining basis and limitations
2. **Evidence Requirement**: 
   - ALL costs: Show calculation (quantity × price = total) + pricing source + date
   - ALL timelines: Show basis (task count, team size, velocity) OR state "TBD pending detailed planning"
   - ALL probabilities: Cite source (historical data, benchmark, expert judgment) OR use qualitative (HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW)
3. **Prohibited Content**:
   - ❌ "$X,XXX" without calculation method
   - ❌ "X months" without task breakdown or basis
   - ❌ "Y% probability" without source
   - ❌ "Similar projects cost..." without specific citation
4. **Required Alternatives** when data missing:
   - Timeline unknown: "Logical sequencing: Phase A must complete before Phase B. Duration TBD based on team velocity"
   - Cost unknown: "Cost estimation requires: [specific usage data]. Recommended: Pilot deployment for baseline"
   - Risk unknown: "Risk cannot be quantified. Mitigation: [specific controls]. Monitoring: [specific metrics]"
5. **Validation Focus**: Emphasize iterative refinement, proof-of-concept validation, and assumption testing

**Quality Criteria**:
- ✅ Every estimate has explicit disclaimer
- ✅ Every numeric value shows calculation or cites source
- ✅ "Unknown" is acceptable if properly explained
- ✅ Focus on strategic direction and priorities, not precision
- ✅ Emphasize decision points and risk mitigation over firm commitments
